# Limit-20 experiment summary

## Provenance and scope

- The supplied experiment CSVs contain exactly 20 distinct image names: `0801.png` through `0820.png` (inclusive).
- The local runner defaults to seed `42` and accepts `--device cuda`; however, the uploaded bundle does not include a run log proving the exact command, selected device, software versions, or checkpoint hashes for these CSVs. - Tables below are regenerated from the per-image CSVs in this archive and replace the stale aggregates previously written in this text file.
